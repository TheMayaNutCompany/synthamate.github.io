WELCOME TO JUPITER ACADEMY OF MUSIC

A place where the music belongs to you.

Every musician starts somewhere. A first note on the piano. A rhythm on the drums. A favorite song finally played from beginning to end.

At Jupiter Academy of Music, we believe those moments matter. We welcome aspiring musicians of different ages and experience levels with thoughtful instruction, an encouraging environment, and room to enjoy the process.

A LIFE IN MUSIC

From Greenwich Village
to Jupiter, Florida.

Our director's musical journey spans stages, countries, classrooms, and generations.

01 / THE BEGINNING

A childhood surrounded by music.

Born and raised in New York City's Greenwich Village, Brad Schechter grew up with music all around him. His father, a professional musician and educator, performed on Broadway and with orchestras throughout the Northeast.

At just seven years old, Brad was already performing alongside his father.

01
GREENWICH VILLAGE NEW YORK CITY
02 / FINDING HIS SOUND

From student to professional performer.

At sixteen, Brad began his first full-time professional engagement at the resort hotels of New York's Catskill Mountains. He later studied music at the State University of New York at New Paltz.

Those formative years turned a childhood passion into a lifelong profession.

02
CATSKILL MOUNTAINS FIRST PROFESSIONAL GIG
03 / ACROSS THE ATLANTIC

Seven years, countless stages.

At twenty-one, Brad joined a rock 'n' roll revue and spent seven years performing throughout Europe. Life on the road sharpened his musicianship and deepened his understanding of the joy that music brings to an audience.

In time, he settled in Switzerland, where he founded his first music school, Brad's Music Academy.

03
JAM
EUROPE & SWITZERLAND PERFORMANCE MEETS TEACHING
04 / A NEW MUSICAL HOME

One community. A new chapter.

In 2009, Brad and his family moved to South Florida. He joined Jupiter Academy of Music's teaching staff and became part of the community of students, families, and fellow musicians.

In 2019, after a decade teaching at JAM, Brad was offered the opportunity to take over the academy as longtime owner Sandra Baran began her retirement.

MEET OUR OWNER & DIRECTOR

Brad Schechter

Performer. Educator. Musician at heart.

Drums were Brad's first musical passion, but his work extends to piano, guitar, bass guitar, and ukulele. His experience as a multi-instrumentalist helps him connect the details of learning an instrument with the larger experience of making music together.

For Brad, the real reward is watching students grow—from their very first lesson to a confident performance onstage.
