GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Can students begin partway through the year?

Yes. JAM’s published scheduling information says lessons can begin at any time during the year, subject to instructor availability.

Do all instruments cost the same?

The prices above are JAM’s published general private-lesson rates. Please confirm pricing for a particular instructor, instrument, group class or special program.

Are there additional times for homeschool students?

Yes. JAM lists additional scheduling options for homeschool students; contact the studio for current availability.

How do cancellations and make-up lessons work?

JAM’s posted registration policies specify advance notice and limits on make-up lessons. Please review the current registration policies with your instructor.

How do I enroll?

Use the lesson request form or call (561) 747-6878. JAM can help match you with an instructor and lesson length.
