Music Lessons That Fit Local Family Life.

JAM welcomes children, teens and adults for private and group instruction, with after-school scheduling, homeschool-friendly options, school-band support and Rock Band opportunities. Instrument availability varies by instructor.

A Local Place to Grow Through Music.

Jupiter Academy of Music is recognized by the community for its commitment to music education. Its mission is to provide exceptional music education and performance opportunities to students of all ages, while helping make music a meaningful part of the Jupiter community.
