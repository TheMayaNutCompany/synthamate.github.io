August Kokus

🐅

August Kokus has been drawn to music from an early age and considers it the most expressive of the arts. He earned his Associate of Arts degree from Palm Beach State College and especially loves the music of Alexander Scriabin—particularly Symphony No. 2 and Symphony No. 3, Le Divin Poème—and Frédéric Chopin, along with fast, energetic classical repertoire. August believes learning piano should be every bit as engaging and fun as playing a great video game. He encourages his students to strengthen their sight-reading, become comfortable with scales, and discover exciting music they might never have encountered on their own. Outside of music, he enjoys botany and growing Maya nut (Brosimum alicastrum) trees. He began piano lessons at age six with JoAnn Floyd at George’s Music in 2009. Years later, their paths came together again at JAM—a connection that brings his musical journey full circle. His father, fellow JAM instructor Gregory Kokus, also teaches at the academy.

Brad Schechter

🥁

Born and raised in New York City, Brad Schechter studied music at the State University of New York at New Paltz before spending seven years touring Europe with a rock-and-roll revue. He later settled in Switzerland, where he founded Brad’s Music Academy in 2000 while maintaining a full teaching schedule and performing in rock, jazz, blues and country bands. Brad has taught students ranging from first-time beginners and teenagers to adults discovering a new hobby. His approach pairs a fun, positive atmosphere with a solid foundation in theory and technique, guided by a simple philosophy: music should be fun. He returned to the United States in 2009 and continued teaching in South Florida. As JAM’s Owner & Director, Brad is also the primary contact for lesson questions and scheduling; call or text 561-747-6878 or email brad@jupiteracademyofmusic.com.

Gary Frost

❄️

Originally from Bennington, Vermont, Gary Frost has worked as a professional musician since 1985. Early road work took him throughout the United States and abroad before he settled in Nashville to focus on songwriting and original acoustic music. After moving to Stuart in 1993, Gary became a familiar presence in the South Florida music scene, performing solo and with groups including Night Train, Big Vince & the Phat Cats and Acoustic Soul. His performance history also includes opening for Dr. John, Loverboy, The Knack and The Fabulous Thunderbirds. As a guitar teacher, Gary believes learning an instrument can build confidence, self-esteem and a connection to the wider music community.

Gregory Kokus

🥥

Gregory Kokus is a multi-instrumentalist and educator with more than 20 years of professional performance and teaching experience. He has logged more than 7,000 performances throughout Palm Beach County, from local clubs and private events to large festivals and touring Broadway theatre productions. Greg has taught students across a wide range of ages and skill levels in both private and school settings. He has served as band director at Palm Beach County Day Academy and has instructed at Dreyfoos School of the Arts. Greg studied at Palm Beach State College and Florida Atlantic University, where he majored in Music Education. His son, fellow JAM instructor August Kokus, also teaches at the academy.

Jim Estes

🎺

Jim Estes has taught trumpet and other brass instruments privately for decades, with students earning notable success in district honor ensembles. His own performance work includes serving as Principal Trumpet with ensembles such as The Academy Orchestra and the South Florida Philharmonic, as well as an appointment as Principal Solo Cornet of the Orchid City Brass Band. Jim holds a Bachelor of Music Education from the University of Southern Mississippi and a master’s degree from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. He was a Graduate Assistant at the University of Miami, completed doctoral coursework and qualifying examinations in Music Education, and is a National Board Certified Teacher.

JoAnn Floyd

🎹

JoAnn Floyd brings decades of piano teaching and professional performance experience to her students. Her love of music began early through the influence of her father, a professional drummer, and by her teenage years she was already performing in bands with him. JoAnn studied music at Suffolk Community College and Hofstra University and studied piano with Blanche Abrams, Mark Hyams and jazz pianist Marian McPartland. She has performed with her husband, guitarist, singer and author Tom Floyd, at hotels, restaurants, country clubs and resorts from Maine to Miami. Throughout her performing career, JoAnn has continued teaching piano students of all ages.

Teresa Olowin

🎹

Teresa Olowin’s musical path began at JAM, where she studied for 11 years, starting private piano lessons at age six and voice lessons at age eight. She earned a bachelor’s degree in Music with a specialization in Music Education from Barry University in 2021 and gained experience teaching both group and private piano. Teresa has taught piano since 2018 in online and in-person settings, with education and practical work that include Kodály- and Gordon-informed methods. Her teaching philosophy emphasizes musical literacy and a strong foundation for applying musical skills rather than simply perfecting a single piece.

Tom Floyd

🥋

Guitarist Tom Floyd has spent decades as a professional musician and teacher. He authored “Quartal Harmony and Voicings for Guitar,” published by Mel Bay, and taught for eight years as an adjunct Professor of Jazz Guitar at Florida Atlantic University. Tom has played recording sessions in Florida and Nashville and studied with guitarists including Pat Martino, Jack Wilkins, Chuck Wayne, Sal Salvador and Don Arnone. He teaches jazz, rock, classical, blues and country, helping each student play the music they want to play while developing a practical understanding of guitar and music theory. His individualized approach encourages students to apply their skills across many forms of music—and to always laugh along the way.
