August Kokus

Piano · Guitar · Bass · Ukulele · Clarinet

Teaches
PianoGuitarBassUkuleleClarinet

August Kokus has been drawn to music from an early age and considers it the most expressive of the arts. He earned his Associate of Arts degree from Palm Beach State College and especially loves the music of Alexander Scriabin—particularly Symphony No. 2 and Symphony No. 3, Le Divin Poème—and Frédéric Chopin, along with fast, energetic classical repertoire. August believes learning piano should be every bit as engaging and fun as playing a great video game. He encourages his students to strengthen their sight-reading, become comfortable with scales, and discover exciting music they might never have encountered on their own. Outside of music, he is an avid student of botany. He began piano lessons at age six with JoAnn Floyd at George’s Music in 2009. Years later, their paths came together again at JAM—a connection that brings his musical journey full circle. His father, fellow JAM instructor Gregory Kokus, also teaches at the academy.

Brad Schechter

Drums · Guitar · Piano · Ukulele · Intro

Teaches
DrumsGuitarPianoUkuleleIntro

Born and raised in New York City, Brad Schechter studied music at the State University of New York at New Paltz before spending seven years touring Europe with a rock-and-roll revue. He later settled in Switzerland, where he founded Brad’s Music Academy in 2000 while maintaining a full teaching schedule and performing in rock, jazz, blues and country bands. Brad has taught everyone from young beginners and teenagers to retirees discovering a new hobby. His approach pairs a fun, positive atmosphere with a solid foundation in theory and technique, guided by a simple philosophy: music should be fun. He returned to the United States in 2009 and continued teaching in South Florida.

Gary Frost

Guitar · Bass · Ukulele

Teaches
GuitarBassUkulele

Originally from Bennington, Vermont, Gary Frost has worked as a professional musician since 1985. Early road work took him throughout the United States and abroad before he settled in Nashville to focus on songwriting and original acoustic music. After moving to Stuart in 1993, Gary became a familiar presence in the South Florida music scene, performing solo and with groups including Night Train, Big Vince & the Phat Cats and Acoustic Soul. His performance history also includes opening for Dr. John, Loverboy, The Knack and The Fabulous Thunderbirds. As a guitar teacher, Gary believes learning an instrument can build confidence, self-esteem and a connection to the wider music community.

Tom Floyd

Guitar

Teaches
Guitar

Guitarist Tom Floyd has spent decades as a professional musician and teacher. He authored “Quartal Harmony and Voicings for Guitar,” published by Mel Bay, and taught for eight years as an adjunct Professor of Jazz Guitar at Florida Atlantic University. Tom has played recording sessions in Florida and Nashville and studied with guitarists including Pat Martino, Jack Wilkins, Chuck Wayne, Sal Salvador and Don Arnone. He teaches jazz, rock, classical, blues and country, helping each student play the music they want to play while developing a practical understanding of guitar and music theory. His individualized approach encourages students to apply their skills across many forms of music—and to always laugh along the way.
